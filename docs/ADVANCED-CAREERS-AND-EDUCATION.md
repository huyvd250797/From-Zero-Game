# V2.10.0 — Advanced Careers & Education
Baseline: uploaded FromZero V2.9.0 Life Planning & Automation. No change to Master Data 3.1.0, scene, map, assets, time advancement, employment schedules, tuition, or save schema 1.

## Labor market and recruitment
`src/career-development.js` derives 2–11 simulated competitor profiles from adult working residents, a deterministic vacancy/day seed and resident needs. They are hypothetical applicants, not actual NPC job changes, and do not reserve slots. The interview threshold is the maximum of the simulated scores and existing base threshold (65 or 72 in recession), bounded by 92. Matching qualifications, location, operating hours, application expiry, daily retry restriction, and economic vacancy gate are still enforced. Score ties meet the threshold. The successful applicant gets an offer with a fixed salary; the score and threshold persist. Hiring still requires explicit acceptance at the employer. Legacy offers without a quoted salary use the previous wage calculation at signing.

## Compensation and career reviews
`salaryOffer` uses the canonical economic wage quote, then premiums: 3% per average skill level above job minimum (max 15%), 2% per 30 documented shifts in that same job (max 12%), 4% per completed program whose skill is relevant (max 8%). Total premium capped at 30%; monetary values rounded to integer VND. New contract and promotion use this quote; existing salary/raise and payroll rules remain unchanged. No automatic job change. Satisfaction starts at 70 (legacy fallback) and changes +3 on good shifts (score >=80), unchanged on 60–79, -5 below 60, -4 additional for lateness, -5 for absence. Clamp 0–100. UI provides development guidance. No arbitrary new dismissal rule from satisfaction.

## Education and career goal
Existing 14 monthly job types, 71 employer/job vacancies, 17 courses, skill and credential catalog preserved. Career goal is optional and persistent. Planner shows rank, skill level/XP gap, canonical predecessor experience, matching and qualification-raising courses accessible by current education rank, economic tuition and duration, related employers and projected salary. Relevant qualifying degree courses prioritized; existing rules do not require a degree to match the profession field beyond rank and skills. No automatic enrollment/graduation; prerequisites remain canonical. Specialization labels derived from XP level: 0 beginner, 1 basic, 2–4 professional, 5–7 proficient, 8–10 expert; these labels are not new official qualifications.

## Automation and save compatibility
`studySkill` optional per study task: a canonical skill ID or `goal`. Old tasks without it default to SKL_IT. Goal mode chooses the largest XP deficit among the target's required skills; when minimum skill levels are reached, the task is marked skipped with reason (education rank/experience still separately required). No goal means no valid skill; it cannot silently study a different skill. Actual library self-study uses original `selfStudy` function and daily 4-hour limit, safety clone, existing travel and budget logic. The planner UI can add a weekly goal-directed study task directly from career goal. New optional save fields: `career.goal`, `career.contract.satisfaction`, `career.application.competition`, `career.application.offerPay`, and `planner.tasks[].studySkill`. Old saves remain accepted. Rollback after updating an offer is not recommended because older code ignores the new offer price and study preference; export before upgrade.
