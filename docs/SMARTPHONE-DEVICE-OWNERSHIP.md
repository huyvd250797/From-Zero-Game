# V3.3.0 — Smartphone & Device Ownership
New characters start with a backpack only. Existing schema-1 saves without `device` convert one existing ITM_PHONE into a used feature phone with 30 days of prepaid service; no charge or fabricated transaction. Extra historical inventory remains inert. Existing phone valuation is replaced by depreciated resale valuation. Save schema remains 1.

Eight fictional models, six existing retail locations. Buy/sell only while near the shop, open, and not driving or in custody. One equipped phone; sell before upgrade. Purchase includes 30 days service. Manual renewal 30/70k base, capped prepayment. Battery decays with simulated game minutes, SMS/call/phone transfer drain charge, charging at occupied home free or shop 10k base, 30 game minutes. No actual SIM, calls or internet. Call is a simulated check-in, shared daily limit with SMS. Prices are in-game balancing values, not actual model quotes. Device value included once in balance sheet; retained like personal items during bankruptcy and valued under existing estate rule. In-game expenses use EXP_SUPPLIES, resale uses INC_LIQUIDATION with clear note.

Smartphones support existing digital applications; data-dependent entry points require active prepaid service. Physical management screens remain accessible independently of phone; their existing physical transaction guards remain. No phone or dead battery opens equipment/store management only. Feature phone can call/SMS and access device management. iOS and Android use different original SVG icon sets and shells, not exact proprietary OS assets; no external dependencies. Samsung models share One UI style, iPhone models share iOS style. Keypad and OS navigation indicators are decorative; actual app actions are buttons. No real device brand affiliation.

Personal finance wallet transfers now require the physical bank counter, or the smartphone banking screen with data access. The canonical transfer ledger remains available to existing internal systems.
