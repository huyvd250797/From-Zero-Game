# V3.5.2 — Organic City & Living River

Baseline: user-provided V3.5.1 ZIP. New derived layout, no changes to canonical master data or asset IDs. Map 384 × 384, 18 districts, 60 interaction anchors. Formal zones: selected central and business/residential corridors retain rectangular layouts; outskirts keep 28 shared housing clusters and L-shaped pedestrian alleys from layout 5. Ten curved diagonal streets connect existing real graph junctions; seven 32-unit local road stretches removed (544 removed world units cumulatively). River-facing roads have 10 new bends. River channel samples both banks at unit intervals with 11 control points, width 10–19 units; previous fixed width 8. Nine waterfront decorative parcels are reallocated; a few other decorative parcels move to clear diagonal roads. No interactive door moved. All seven roundabouts and twelve crossings retained.

## Water rendering
`river-layout.js` is the shared geometry authority: continuous piecewise-linear banks, collision trapezoids clipped at actual bridge windows, exact segment clipping. `river-render.js` builds river depth-gradient surfaces, embankments and bridge rails, with native moving wave ribbons. `three-river.js` renders a single indexed mesh, using procedural GLSL waves, analytic normal gradients, daylight and view-dependent glints, and bank/depth colour. No downloaded textures or AI concept artwork. This is a lightweight visual model, not a physical fluid solver, SSR, dynamic mirrored buildings or navigable boat simulation. Time is visual elapsed time while the renderer is active, independent of the in-game simulation clock. Reduced motion disables wave travel. Hidden app and idle title remain governed by existing render scheduler.

## Compatibility and limitations
Layout marker 6; saves without marker or layout 4/5 are validated against their corresponding old footprint and straight-water barrier before relocation. Schema 1, no SQL migration. All canonical IDs and interactive anchors retained. 24 inherited axes remain as a structural skeleton; ten new diagonal links and the shoreline corridor were redesigned in this release. The image reference is an art direction, not a promise of identical photorealism. Some ordinary buildings still lie in rectangular blocks and have four neighboring streets; no comprehensive plot-by-plot frontage redesign. New diagonal roads are included in automatic routes and static maps, but ambient traffic follows inherited lane axes and dedicated lane/signal behavior for diagonal junctions is not yet implemented. Advanced lane AI, road hierarchy, highways, and public transport upgrades remain in their roadmap versions. Existing shoreline crossings are retained rather than adding major elevated bridge structures.
