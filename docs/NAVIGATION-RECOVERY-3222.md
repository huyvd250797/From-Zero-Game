# V3.22.2 — Intelligent Navigation & Traffic Recovery

- Preserve destinations on temporary blockage; retry at most once per second. Walking detours use a bounded visibility graph around nearby blockers and verify continuous segment, motorway access and collision clearance. Manual movement tries alternate directions. The target is not silently teleported past obstacles.
- Visible scheduled pedestrians use bounded presentation recovery. Authoritative schedules remain time-indexed; offscreen and transitions into buildings are not fully physically simulated. Visual separation is local, not a new authoritative NPC scheduler.
- Anticipatory braking checks the actual lane corridor; actual collision sweeps still protect pedestrians. Buses include pedestrians in physical sweeps.
- Only straight urban avenues/boulevards with adjacent same-direction lanes are candidates for automatic bypass; new recovery does not intentionally override red signals. Physical clearance remains mandatory. No guarantee of progress on fully blocked roads. Scripted vehicle avoidance uses kinematic lateral interpolation rather than full steering physics; signal and collision integration on browser/Three.js remains unverified.
- Scope does not add a new global NavMesh, global dynamic route replanning for traffic loops, parking maneuver overhaul, or emergency priority hierarchy (V3.22.3). Save schema, master data, traffic budget and GLB unchanged.
- Acceptance: walk toward a parked car or pedestrian; confirm alternate route reaches original target. Stand on the bus curb, then cross safely; traffic should distinguish these cases. Block a same-direction lane on a straight avenue, test free/occupied adjacent lane and red light. On a narrow road with no clearance the vehicle must wait. Test player bus boarding and resident traffic during peak hour. See docs/qa/V3.22.2-RESULTS.txt and docs/RELEASE-GATE.md.
