# V1.9.0 — Immersive Activities

## Player experience
- Enter the existing 3D interior and reach a station. The existing direct action opens an animated activity scene using the same procedural room geometry and its selected furniture. Shop, kitchen, inventory, formal job, self-study/course attendance and social menus also launch the activity scene on eligible actions. For outdoor actions or places without a supported interior, a small illustrative local scene is used instead of a new city scene. Walking follows a bounded visual loop; exterior game position does not change.
- Close-up camera by default; Toàn phòng/Cận cảnh toggles framing. Activity controls: ×1, ×2, ×4, Hoàn thành ngay and Hủy hoạt động. The top close button or Esc discards unfinished activity. Explicit cancellation returns to interior or original menu where applicable. Reduced-motion setting suppresses the player movement/pose cycle. Activity animation pauses in background.
- Eight additional procedural skeletal clips: sleep, drink, cook, wash, exercise, deskwork, deskread, seatdrink. Existing sit, eat, work, walk, shop and talk are reused. Bed and chair anchors are visual only; props (cup, food, book, utensil, shopping package) are attached to right forearm. Canvas transforms the same geometry; Three.js uses Bone and AnimationMixer. No motion capture, soft skinning, fluid effects, cloth simulation, or seamless city animation.

## Transaction semantics
`ActivityRun` owns only transient presentation state. Preview lasts approximately 3–10 seconds at ×1. Game clock remains paused; progress reports planned simulation minutes, not elapsed save time. Natural completion or skip revalidates action, save token and active state, then invokes the original business operation once. The operation advances canonical time and applies cash/items/XP/needs once. Cancel, close or reload before commit discards pending action; no partial reward, partial time or new save schema. No new persistence key, no Master Data migration. Most non-activity menus retain their existing behavior. Formal work, attendance, self-study, purchases, cooking, item use, social actions and selected location activities use their unchanged existing rules. A rejected operation never retries automatically.
