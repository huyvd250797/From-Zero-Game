# V3.22.0 — Education & School Life

## Education
Four decorative lots are converted to kindergarten, primary, lower-secondary and upper-secondary schools; two existing training sites are identified as college and university/training center. All six have visible signs, opening hours, walkable doors and public classroom layouts. The existing canonical 17 courses, degree conditions and Master Data are retained. Use **Học tập & kỹ năng → Trường học** (or interact with a school) to see occupancy and enroll children. Age bands 3–5, 6–10, 11–14, 15–17 and one-time enrollment fees 250k, 120k, 160k, 220k VND are game parameters. School attendance is settled at the family daily update on weekdays, subject to care, health and household debt; older saves with no enrollment retain legacy education progression. At a new age band enroll again. The optional school field is validated in schema 1. No exams, school-year grading, independent teaching career or separate child avatar commute tied to a player's child yet.

## City residents
65 canonical named residents retain saved finances, relations and daily decisions. 180 background residents in year one (10 per district, previously 90) have deterministic IDs, home, work/school, occupation, wealth tier and timed itinerary, including weekday/weekend and selected lunch trips. The year-based cohort is five above each district's former background count. Residents are hidden from the outdoor renderer at indoor destinations; selected indoor occupants are visible in public room layouts (up to three). The background group has no individual persisted money/account, and generic traffic actors remain a separate system. No new density of generic 32-actor traffic pool is requested. Vehicle choice uses pre-owned archetypes, cash/needs and distance for named residents; background uses deterministic archetypes. Road paths and bus itineraries are sampled with fractional minutes, not random back-and-forth decorations. Taxi is a simulated road leg without pickup/payment. Private vehicle parking is visual allocation near the destination, not a full door-to-parking maneuver and dismount animation; traffic holds may cause mismatch with the planned clock, especially delayed bus transfers. Not every background resident is visible at once: indoor residents are removed from the street, outside background cap 56 desktop high, 14 Eco, 12 mobile normal / 8 cool or pressure 1 / 4 pressure 2. Separate traffic/vehicle/character budgets remain.

## Police visuals
CB250P-inspired rider and motorcycle details rebuilt in original geometry: rounded helmet/face/torso, elbows and knees, shoulder tabs, belt, boots, fairing, storage and emergency lights. Three LODs; not a professional OEM asset. Lux patrol sedan unchanged. No new GLB or data migration.
