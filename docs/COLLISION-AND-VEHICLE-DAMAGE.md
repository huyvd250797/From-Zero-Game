# V3.10.0 — Collision & Vehicle Damage

Baseline V3.9.0, layout 9, save schema 1, Three.js r180. No new database, canonical location or environment variable. Old saves default to undamaged vehicles.

- Player-operated owned vehicles use swept chassis discs against the most recently rendered traffic, scheduled buses, resident vehicles, pedestrian samples, parked owned vehicles and spatially indexed roadside tree/lamp proxies. Existing movement sweep handles buildings, islands, water and boundaries. Collisions stop the player vehicle, estimate relative kinetic impact energy using profile masses, record damage and affect player health. Contact cooldown limits repeated damage. Ambient traffic and transit remain scheduled, not rigid-body actors; no general NPC-to-NPC collision simulation or persistent NPC injuries.
- Five condition values (body, engine, wheels, glass, lights), 60 saved accident records, reduced performance and breakdown. Mesh deformation and windshield marks are lightweight approximations; no per-panel rigid-body deformation. Critical thresholds: engine <=8, wheels <=5, body <=3.
- Vehicle menu → Repair & recovery. Existing showroom (Cửa hàng phương tiện) serves as garage; repairs require proximity, opening hours, available funds and 30 game minutes. Towing moves vehicle and player to the garage after paid 20-game-minute service; no animated tow truck or towing physics yet. Paid scheduled maintenance restores some engine and wheel condition. No insurance or legal penalty system in this release.
- Vehicle lamp sources are collected by the common renderer, with directional front light, red tail/brake, reverse lamps and condition-dependent failure. Parked vehicles are unlit. Three.js uses additive soft footprints and unlit lens meshes for up to 192 lamp sources plus bounded spotlights for nearby headlights; headlight operation does not depend on weather-effects preference. Native preview uses simpler colored meshes and light patches, not physically based lighting. Actual Three.js browser rendering needs verification.
- Source tests cover swept collisions, impact energy, damage persistence, save validation, degradation, repair/tow transactions, lamp positions, native dynamic geometry and visual damage geometry.
