# V3.5.3 — Natural Urban Masterplan & Realistic Water

## Design and runtime
- Layout 7, reconstructed in game geometry from the approved visual direction. Not a raster image or a pixel-exact tracing of the concept. Original master records are unchanged; the runtime replatting layer resolves geometry, doors and assets by stable IDs.
- All 24 underlying axial road references remain, but 889.07 world units of road intervals are cut where the river, its two bank roads or a replacement diagonal requires it. No short axial sine bends. Five diagonal connectors, two long peripheral arcs, and two continuous riverbank roads use explicit polylines. Five roundabouts remain. Every at-grade centerline intersection becomes a shared graph vertex. Existing signal AI remains axis-oriented; new diagonal junction yielding and dedicated lane logic are future work.
- The river centreline uses cubic interpolation, with both banks offset about the same centre; 385 samples, 568.38-unit centreline arc length, approximately 20–25-unit horizontal channel width. It enters the bottom/east portion and meanders strongly west through the southern/middle area before heading to the north/east edge. Four bridge crossings at Z=80,144,272,336 replace twelve. The water barrier uses exact trapezoidal clipping. The map, native mesh, shader, collision and road graph use shared geometry. Bridge approaches remain flat in this release.
- All 329 site IDs, 306 solid buildings, 60 interaction IDs, and 18 logical districts remain. 28 complete shared residential clusters (3/4/6/8 homes) are relocated together, with reserved orthogonal access paths. Replotting changes 244 footprint positions or dimensions, including 19 interaction sites. All 60 interaction sites stay in their original logical district. The existing district boundaries are not redrawn as geographic polygons; no new property ownership IDs. 85 alley segments, 13 retained landscaped yards; riverbank green strips and embankments follow the banks.
- Animated water: arc-length flow coordinate, multi-frequency waves, depth tint, specular sunlight, Fresnel-like sky tint, bank foam and rain ripple approximation. Native preview uses moving wave ribbons and a colored surface; no external textures, no video. Real-time wave clock remains independent of game speed. No physically simulated fluid, planar building reflections, or verified GPU realism.
- City map uses subdued district shading, river outline, road markings, continuous shore roads and no default dashed district grid. All original HUD and gameplay modules retained.

## Save migration
Save schema 1 remains. Layout 7 accepts unmarked/4/5/6 saves after validating against their corresponding original layouts (layout 6 uses the original river and old planner). Positions at moved interaction doors are relocated to the new door. Other invalidated positions are relocated to the nearest valid ground. Riding/active vehicle position stays synchronized. Original input is not mutated. Money, inventory, household, phone, business and ownership identifiers are unchanged. Back up JSON before updating; do not downgrade a layout 7 save directly into V3.5.2.
