# V1.6.0 — Living NPC World

## Movement and behavior
Previously `npcWorld(state.minutes,state)` was sampled at integer simulation minutes in geometry, labels and Three.js actors. At 1×, clock configuration is one real second per game minute, so NPC roots stayed still for almost a second then jumped. `NpcPresentation` now samples `state.minutes + simSeconds` from `main.js` each render. Same snapshot for Three.js, native renderer, Canvas and labels. Route geometry remains canonical; yaw interpolates on shortest arc; CPU gait phase advances with actual displayed distance and Three.js walk clip rate is bounded 0.1–6. Pausing freezes NPC frames and mixers; large clock jumps reset instead of traversing skipped routes. At high simulation speed, faster movement is expected.

`livingSnapshot` derives work/read/eat/shop/rest/home/walk from existing schedules, and pairs nearby residents in leisure periods for conversation. Local park/garden/sport strolls have maximum 0.72 world-unit deviation. Personal-space offsets limited to 0.42 world units, checked against building collision and smoothed across frames. Companion markers are assigned to nearby commuters headed toward the same venue and direction, not a group pathfinding system. This is visual avoidance, not guaranteed collision-free crowd physics. Named NPCs retain their canonical identity, contacts, routes and social availability. Authoritative interaction still checks canonical integer-minute rendezvous position; visual root can differ slightly, especially during fast-forward. Slow down to meet a moving NPC.

Public room roster derived from venue and opening hours at entry, up to three residents. Non-station, non-entry walkable cells near matching furniture; independent gestures, no blocking of player routes. Roster rebuilt on re-entry and after direct public activities. Outside positions remain rendezvous points; indoor NPC representation does not create economic transactions, inventory, relationships or separate agents. Family room models remain static; no new AI family simulation. No additional population records or cross-day autonomous progression. Gestures are procedural, no props, physical eating or seated furniture alignment. Indoor movement currently stationary gesture poses.
