# From Zero V2.11.0 — Business & Wealth Simulation

## Scope
Upgrade V2.10.0 in place. Six canonical enterprise types, one company, up to two leased branches. Master Data 3.1.0 (1,028 records) and schema 1 unchanged. All existing characters, housing, skills, contracts and automation retained.

## Operating model
- Open a branch at a compatible existing place after upgrading the business: level 2 permits one branch, level 3 permits two. Each costs 35% of current startup capital, expensed immediately; no refund on closure. Every branch adds the same fixed rent as HQ. All use the existing shared wallet, weighted-cost inventory, product/price, and revenue/cost totals. Staff automatically allocate one per branch in opening order, then HQ. Each staffed branch increases the demand multiplier by 0.60. Market competition uses the existing macroeconomic demand and competition factors; no independent rival-company accounts or site-specific stock/sales.
- HR tab shows staffing capacity, allocation, role and market-linked payroll. Hire/fire through existing functions and pay the first day's wage when hired. Staff are aggregate simulated employees, not named citizens transferred from their jobs. Payroll and fixed rent accrue at midnight, including unpaid obligations; debt blocks new operations.
- Supply offers standard (100% reference cost, minimum 1), local (97%, minimum 20), wholesale (92%, minimum 100). Orders delivered immediately into the shared warehouse, no lead-time or supply disruption simulation. Weighted inventory cost, not an operating expense until sold. Automation policies optional: replenish below threshold to target if lot minimum, funds, minimum reserve and next-day overhead are covered. No automatic personal funding, borrowing or profit withdrawal.
- Staff operation disabled by default. When explicitly enabled at HQ, begins the next full day; at midnight after overhead, staff sell for the previous day if owner did not already operate, adequate staff (one per site including HQ), stock, reserve, no debt, no imprisonment, restriction or life ending. Demand is 75% of staff-run reference demand (without owner health/age penalty); management skill and marketing still apply. Owner work/skill XP not credited. Automatic sales remain in the business. Reorder is independent and can be enabled without staffed operation. Stop button disables both from anywhere. Only advances with in-game clock, not real-world background. After reload enabled business policies remain configured, unlike personal planner. No guaranteed profit, no guarantee that all product sales correspond to actual city shoppers.

## Accounting
7/30/90-day ledger of recognized sales, cost of goods sold, accrued payroll and fixed overhead, other expenses, stock purchases, contributions, withdrawals, actual fees paid, and company-paid operating debt. P&L = sales - COGS - accrued payroll - accrued overhead - other costs. Cash flow = sales - purchases - paid fees - other costs + contributions - withdrawals - company-paid debt. Both are company-only; existing lifetime totals retained. Coverage starts with first new recorded transaction for legacy company; no invented historical breakdown. Recognition at midnight for automatic sales and accrued fees. At most 91 transaction days retained. No interest or income tax modeled in company report, personal loan obligations still tracked separately. Liquidation still values stock at 70% and retains unpaid debts; all branches close with the company. No branch resale value or additional assets counted.

## Investments & wealth
Activate existing INV_LAND asset; at bank buy/sell up to 5 lots, using current simulated property-price multiplier. Every 30-game-day boundary pays 0.25% of market value per lot into personal cash as INC_RENT if entire holding has been held for at least 30 days since most recent purchase. No income backfill; duplicate settlement protected. No actual property location, tenant management or new residential deed. Land is distinct from player's home. Portfolio marked to market, land liquidation through original bankruptcy flow, estate calculation includes portfolio. Gross purchase/sale cash flows follow existing game reporting; realized gain is tracked separately. Wealth tab reconciles cash, account, term savings, portfolio, enterprise (cash + stock), other property, receivables and debts with canonical balance sheet. Monthly passive income = interest + dividends + rent + pension; withdrawn business profit shown separately. All financial values are fictional gameplay values, not real investment products or projections.

## Save and deployment
Optional `business.active.management` and `banking.rentReceived` / `lastRentAt` accepted by schema 1. Old active business stays valid; records are initialized on first new accounting event. Back up JSON before upgrading; rollback can fail when holding land or lose branch settings. No database migration. Vercel Other, Node 22, npm ci, npm run build, dist. Three.js r180 unchanged and production gate remains enabled. Native preview uses fallback renderer and must not deploy as production.
