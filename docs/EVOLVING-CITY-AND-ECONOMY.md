# V2.13.0 — Evolving City & Economy

## Model
The economy uses 30-day periods; an economic year is 12 periods (360 game days), not the Gregorian calendar year. A deterministic bounded yearly cohort model derives district statistics from the game clock. Year index 0 retains the baseline 5 ambient actors per district and all baseline economic quotes. No wall-clock dependency, extra RNG, network API, save migration or SQL. History can be reconstructed when an old save is opened; it is model history, not transactions recorded from the player's previous release.

Each district has 3–9 ambient actors, 1–30 synthetic establishments, development index 65–150 (100 baseline), demand .8–1.3, competition .75–1.35 and property coefficient .85–1.15. Annual opening and closing counts reconcile to prior stock. These are aggregate statistical businesses, not additional physical buildings or individual AI firms. All 29 named employers and 71 vacancy IDs remain canonical; annual hiring states can close all or some vacancies. Existing contracts are not terminated automatically; existing offers and internal promotion rules remain. Year-zero hiring is unchanged. Actual salary offers and the job requirement gate use the evolving market. The 65 named NPCs remain, with wages and core living expenses indexed from the second economic year; pensions remain as before. Ambient pedestrian counts change in the world; routes use collision-tested segments and existing per-preset rendering budgets (8/16/24/32, Eco 8).

Four economic shocks from month index 12, selected deterministically every six periods and active for two periods. They modify CPI, supply, activity, demand, unemployment, property and stock indices, with a separate multiplier for tech/bank. Existing canonical economic stages, wage index, base interest rates and loan/deposit contracts are retained. CPI is bounded at 1–2; relative inflation compares with 12 economic periods earlier. Price variation is a game model, not a real-world forecast. Supply/input and fuel costs use the same quote functions as gameplay. Business sales use a district demand/competition factor, weighted across staffed branches. Property purchase, valuation and sale use the same local factor; land investment remains the citywide quote. Old collateral purchase prices may match either the original or the evolved quote for the historical purchase date, preserving valid pre-upgrade loans. No arbitrary historic values accepted.

## UI
Menu → Kinh tế thành phố: overview (economic event, population, inflation), prices including four investments, labor, monthly history, districts, enterprises and 12-year history. Map selection shows district development information. Significant economic shocks enter important journal once at the month boundary; ordinary annual and monthly summaries remain in internal journal. No new geometry, demolition, road expansion, true business competitor accounts, migration of named NPCs, or persistent intergenerational city time (next-generation reset is inherited).

## QA
11 new unit tests cover annual stock-flow, 203-year bounds, ambient routes, rendering budgets, shocks, actual quotes, vacancy gate, sector price, legacy collateral, clock transition, NPC accounting. Existing production build gate remains mandatory. Native preview is QA only, not production. Real GPU/browser verification must be reported separately.
